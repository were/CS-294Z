## Academic Writing

- Why it is different from technical writing
  - Technical writing is about conveying your experience to others, and make a public knowledge base for further maintenance and development.
- Acaedmic writing is intended to be vacous so that people may appreciate.
  - Sell your ideas in an impressive way
  - Promote your work and hype the future impact
- Although we always say the technical contribution of a paper is balah..
  - It is more about what you did unique to what it was before, not how you beatifully implemented it.
  - More on the insights you have to solve the problem.
- A typical flow of an academic paper
  - Why the area is important
  - What is the problem you are trying to improve in this area
  - Why prior works failed to solve the problem
  - What is your proposed solution to the problem
    - What is your trade-off: What do you sacrifice to achieve the improvement?
    - What is your design decision: Why do you make this design decision?
    - How do you implement your solution?
      - NOTE: This is still NOT a technical report. No engineering details.
      - Still only key insights and high-level design.
  - How do you evaluate your solution?
    - What is the platform and environment you use to evaluate your solution?
    - Do you have data to support your design decision?
    - Ablation study: How each mechanism individually contributes to the improvement?
      - This is actually a bullshit, which makes your codebase a mess (at least before LLM coding era)

Reviewers/readers have an expectation.
